How Voice Dictation Works:

When enabled, a floating 🎙️ Voice Assistant button appears on the Church Report edit page. Reporters simply speak phrases like "General Tithe fifty thousand naira" or "Minister Allowance thirty-five thousand", and the AI automatically focuses and fills the exact input box without typing.
